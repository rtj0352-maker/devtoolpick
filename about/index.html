About DevToolPick

DevToolPick is an independent English-language site focused on AI coding assistantsand adjacent developer SaaS. We write for working developers and small teams who need clear answers: which tool fits which workflow, what it actually costs, and what trade-offs matter.

Editorial approach

  • Buyer-intent first: roundups, head-to-heads, and deep reviews over generic listicles.
  • Pricing honesty: we cite official pages and note that plans change frequently.
  • No fake benchmarks: we do not invent scores or synthetic “tests” we did not run.
  • Clear disclosures: affiliate relationships are labeled when present.

What we cover

AI-native editors (Cursor, Devin Desktop / Windsurf), IDE extensions (GitHub Copilot), terminal agents (Claude Code), and related developer tooling that affects how you ship software.
